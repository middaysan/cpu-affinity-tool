## 1.7.2 - 2026-10-28

### Fixed
- Fixed System Tweaks being unavailable after upgrading with older saved settings.
- Added **Update saved settings**, with clear progress, error details and retry actions.
- Preserved apps, groups, tracked process names and Undo information during the update.

### Notes
If prompted, open **System Tweaks** and click **Update saved settings**. Opening the page does not change the file. Older app versions may not read updated settings.

### Download
Use **cpu-affinity-tool.exe** below. The matching **cpu_affinity_tool.pdb** is optional for troubleshooting. Windows x64; administrator approval required.
